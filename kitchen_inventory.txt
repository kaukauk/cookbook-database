SUBSTITUTIONS IN USE
  Cayenne        <- grind dried red chiles
  Chaat masala   <- amchur + ground cumin + salt, roughly 2:1:1
  Fresh curry leaves <- dried; add to hot oil with the tempering,
                        not to the simmering liquid
  Double cream   <- HOMEMADE CASHEW CREAM, 1:1, always. Soak 60 g
                    cashews in hot water 30 min, drain, blend with
                    120 ml fresh water until silky (~150 ml). Made
                    in the prep session before the week's dishes.

MADE AT HOME FROM MILK / SCRATCH -- never put these on a shopping
list; list the raw input instead and schedule the prep
  Dahi (yoghurt)  <- whole milk + 1 tbsp live yoghurt as starter.
                     ~1:1 by weight. Covers raita and buttermilk
                     (buttermilk = 100 g dahi + 60 ml water).
  Paneer          <- whole milk + acid. ~1 litre milk per 120 g.
  Cashew cream    <- see above; replaces double cream.
  Tomato paste    <- when a recipe needs it: ~300 g tomatoes cooked
                     down and sieved gives 2-3 tbsp. Freeze spares.
  All of these are made in the PREP SESSION ahead of the dishes
  that need them, and the plan must say which day.

FREEZER / TINS (added 6 Oct 2026)

  coconut milk
  Fresh curry leaves are never bought -- dried only.
